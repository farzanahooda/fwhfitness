Quinoa and Avocado Salad

July 27, 2021 · 1 min read
← Back to Recipes

This salad is so zingy and delicious you’ll want more and more!

Benefits of Quinoa

  • High in protein with a wide range of amino acid profile hence, good for vegetarians and vegans
  • High in fibre
  • Good source of manganese which is required for a healthy metabolism
  • Good source of antioxidants
  • Good source of iron, essential for good health
  • Good source of carbohydrate

Serves 4 as a side

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups cooked cooled quinoa (cook according to packet instructions)
  • 1 chopped cucumber
  • 1 chopped carrot
  • 1 chopped yellow/red pepper
  • Very Large handful of fresh mint
  • Very Large handful of fresh coriander/parsley
  • 1-2 ripe avocado depending on size
  • Pomegranate seeds
  • Handful pumpkin seeds
  • Handful of chopped walnuts
  • Cracked black pepper
  • Pinch of rock salt
  • Can add black beans too for an added fibre, nutrient boost

Dressing:

  • Juice of lemon
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2-3 tbsp of organic extra virgin olive oil (use a good oil)
  • Zaatar
  • Cracked black pepper

Method:

  1. Mix all the ingredients together and add the dressing to it and stir.
  2. Serve immediately
