Cashew Lemon Bliss Ball

January 12, 2023 · 1 min read
← Back to Recipes

You can’t beat a cashew lemon bliss ball and a delicious cup of tea!

Honestly these are heavenly on the taste buds.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of cashew nuts
  • 1 cups soft dates
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Squeeze of half lemon
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • ⅓ cup of oats

Method:

  • Blend all in a mixer
  • Roll into balls
  • Refrigerate

Let me know if you make these! I bet you'll be hooked :).
