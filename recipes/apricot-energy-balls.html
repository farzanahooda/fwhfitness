Apricot Energy balls

July 27, 2021 · 1 min read
← Back to Recipes

Apricots are a great source of vitamins, minerals and antioxidants. They’re rich in Iron and also contain high levels of Vitamin C, Vitamin A, Magnesium (has calming qualities) and fibrous- NHS.

I always choose the unsulphured dried Apricots which are a rich brown colour and not the yellow ones that you usually find.

They are also super sweet so you don’t need much to hit the sweet spot.

Ingredients:

  • 1 ½ cup (organic if you can) unsulphured dried apricots
  • ½ cup raisins
  • ¼ cup almonds
  • ¼ cup walnuts- great for the brown
  • 3 tbsp desiccated unsweetened coconut
  • 1 tbsp @linwoods flax and goji berry mix
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • ½ tsp sweet cinnamon powder
  • 1tbsp coconut oil - optional (helps to bind a little better)

Method:

  1. Transfer all the ingredients in a food processor.
  2. Mix until all combined.
  3. Roll the mix into small balls.
  4. Roll into desiccated coconut if you wish.
  5. Leave in the fridge and enjoy 😊
