About Me

Hi, I'm Farzana

Founder of FWH - Fitness, Wellbeing and Health. Devoted mother of four, wife, and passionate women's health specialist, personal trainer and holistic wellness coach.

After graduating in Economics & Business and working in marketing and project management, I found my true calling in nutrition, movement and supporting women through every stage of life - building strength, balancing hormones, or simply feeling at home in their bodies again.

With a passion for Ayurvedic nutrition and a deep understanding of female physiology, I take a completely bespoke approach - tailoring your training, nutrition and lifestyle around you, your routine, and your stage of life. Because if it doesn't fit into your life, it won't last.
