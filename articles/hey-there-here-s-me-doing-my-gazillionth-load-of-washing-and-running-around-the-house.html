How to increase your metabolism the easy way.

November 25, 2020 · 2 min read
← Back to Articles

Here’s me doing my gazillionth load of washing and running around the house!

But no this is not a post about me doing the housework, but about how you can increase your metabolic activity at home if you're feeling the pounds creeping on.

Simply moving more and sitting less can boost your health dramatically ⭐️.

😀 Even trivial-seeming moves like folding laundry, doing the housework and brushing your teeth help counter the negative effects of sitting for long stretches. This is thanks to “ N on Exercise Activity Thermogenesis ”, otherwise known as NEAT , which is the scientific term for the way everyday activities stimulate your metabolism .

Here’s some simple ways you can increase your NEAT whilst being at home.

  1. Reduce your sitting time by setting an alarm on your phone to remind you to stretch at your desk for a few minutes every hour or walk around your house. ⏰
  2. Take the stairs whenever possible and use the upstairs washroom if you have one to get extra steps in. 🦶
  3. Amp up the calorie burn by dancing whilst doing the housework. 💃🏻
  4. Toe tapping and raising your heels while seated are not only NEAT exercises, they also work the muscles in the lower legs. 🦵
  5. Sit on a stability/exercise ball rather than a chair whilst working. It forces you to subtly contract lots of different muscles to maintain your balance. Or stand and work. 🧘🏽‍♀️
  6. Everyone has a morning routine that they go through. This is an easy time to add some extra movement to your day. Eg after brushing teeth, waiting for the kettle to boil. Implement some form of exercises- calf raises/push ups/burpees. Make it a habit. 🤸🏽‍♀️
  7. Whilst watching TV 📺 , always make it a habit to do some stretches and exercises at the same time. Don’t just lie down or sit.
  8. Get a fitness tracker to measure your steps. Compete with a friend. Nothing like a little healthy competition to get you moving.

💫 Focus on a goal and have fun with it. Find joy 😃 in movement and live a longer, healthier more fulfilling life 🌈🤸🏽‍♀️ 🏊‍♀️🚵🏽‍♀️🏋🏽‍♂️
